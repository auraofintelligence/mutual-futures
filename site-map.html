Public proposal
  1. Start hereA public workbench connecting business succession to mutual ownership, universal intelligence, more possible lives and a future humanity can keep exploring.
  2. Business successionExisting teams. Existing clients. Productive assets and knowledge worth carrying forward. The question is who owns the next chapter.
  3. Mutual wealthMutual wealth has substance when the people involved understand what they own, how decisions are made, who carries risk and what remains for those who come next.
  4. Capital & financeSuccession capital, operating resilience and transition investment are different requirements. A purchase that consumes all three leaves little space for the intended transformation.
  5. Work & lifeTry Everything Once and Intermittent Retirement connect stable participation with the freedom to learn across sectors, change direction, travel, care and rest.
  6. Intelligence & incomeLuke's proposal pairs universal intelligence with universal adequate income. The aim is not merely access to software, or a new label for one predetermined cash-payment model.
  7. Aura & intelligenceAura of Intelligence is the personal layer. Business, civic and scientific systems can connect to it without silently acquiring a person's inner life.
  8. Legal reflectionRadical overcompliance is Luke's proposed transition method: operate within current obligations, make their effects legible, and use lawful processes to examine change.
  9. Simulation studioA proposed society-wide simulation architecture connects what keeps life operating with the people, AI and machines that can act, adapt and recover.
  10. Health & careThe health surge belongs to a wider life, not a requirement that people optimise themselves for an employer, a civic platform or an AI system.
  11. Existential resilienceThe ambition includes helping humanity survive existential threats. The practical question is which capabilities support ordinary life, withstand disruption and preserve the ability to recover.
  12. Kardashev galactic gamesKardashev galactic games is Luke's expansive horizon: humanity developing the capability to explore, cooperate, create and continue beyond today's planetary limits.
  13. World travelTravel, residencies, research, relationships, creativity and time without an agenda belong inside the life design. They do not all need to justify themselves through productivity.
  14. Oceania & Native NationsThe wider pattern is offered for locally chosen participation. Queensland, Oceania, Native Nations and international partners are not branches of one founder's authority.
  15. Culture & world stageFilm, music, art, gatherings and shared stories make the work legible. They also have value beyond explaining a financial or technical programme.
  16. Development horizonsThe architecture reaches from an operating business to long-lived civilisation. Its milestones distinguish present work, proposed next steps and horizons dependent on choices not yet made.
  17. Document libraryTen supplied working papers and submissions connect the wider project family. Guides identify relevant passages, original filenames, status and unresolved claims rather than silently rewriting the source documents.
  18. Sources & connectionsProject sources describe the proposal. Official and scientific sources answer different factual questions. Neither category substitutes for the other.
  19. About this workbenchA standalone public project by Luke Nathan Hayes / Strange But True / Aura of Intelligence, developed from Minjerribah and South East Queensland with an outward-looking horizon.
