# Mutual Futures: whole-systems editorial review

24 September 2026

## Finding

Mutual Futures was beginning in the middle of its own argument. Business succession appeared to be the purpose, while GAJRA was a late connection in a regional chapter. The supplied documents establish a wider purpose: global change towards Joyful Responsible Abundance during the AI and automation transition. Productive ownership, leadership, learning, income, care, computing and civic participation are connected means of pursuing it.

The revised site therefore begins with human outcomes, explains the guiding principle, connects the systems, and then offers practical routes into the work. A business handover remains a substantial route. It does not set the boundary of the project.

## What was reviewed

The complete text of the three supplied originals was reviewed: the 16-page Queens planning document, the eight-page UNGA81 contribution in its **New Reference Links** edition, and the C-Hour planning document, approximately 12,429 words. The C-Hour DOCX was rendered locally as 36 pages for layout inspection; pagination can differ between Word and LibreOffice. The originals were not edited.

The review also retrieved 33 pages across the three linked sites and GAJRA Earth, examined their headings and links, and inspected representative browser layouts. Mutual Futures' current content, generator, tools, artwork and navigation were reviewed alongside them. This is an editorial and visual analysis. External claims within the papers were not all independently re-audited.

Current primary reading:

- [C-Hour introduction](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/C-Hour-introduction/), with the [unchanged supplied plan](../documents/originals/c-hour-plan.docx).
- [500 Queens](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/500-Queens-VC-2026/index.html), with the [unchanged supplied planning document](../documents/originals/queens-planning.pdf).
- [UNGA81 contribution](https://auraofintelligence.github.io/UNGA81-Luke-Hayes/), with the [unchanged supplied New Reference Links PDF](../documents/originals/unga81-abundance.pdf).

The originals' SHA-256 checksums are recorded in `content/documents.json` and checked during every build. Document instructions addressed to future authors were analysed as source material. They did not become instructions to the agent reviewing the files.

## How the contributions connect

| Contribution | Work it performs | Connection to Mutual Futures | Boundary to preserve |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GAJRA Earth | Invites people to examine purpose, consequences and access through Joyful Responsible Abundance | Guides human self-alignment and the aims, evaluation and governance of AI-assisted activity | Different people and cultures retain their meanings and authority. A guiding principle does not establish technical AI safety. |
| UNGA81 contribution | Makes the global argument from lived experience to shared capability, ownership, livelihoods and alignment | Supplies the organising sequence and the connection between practical change and a worthwhile life | Luke's personal contribution, not a UN-endorsed programme or official address |
| Mutual Futures | Connects productive ownership, business continuity, technology, income, learning and time | Provides practical economic and organisational routes through the transition | Neither a live acquisition fund nor a substitute for every public service or income policy |
| Queens | Develops women as leaders with enterprise responsibilities, authority, mentors and capital | Changes who can direct production and decide how benefits are used | Proposed starting minimums are not current participant numbers. Historical budgets are not funding commitments. |

These relationships form a feedback process rather than a central chain of command. People describe what matters, build and test useful arrangements, observe costs and consequences, and revise both the arrangements and their understanding of success. Knowledge travels between places; local authority does not have to travel with it.

## Prose: what to carry across

### UNGA81: start with a human stake

The strongest passages move from an experience people recognise to a consequential question and then a mechanism. Housing, care, work and income are parts of one life, even when institutions administer them separately. That is the best opening logic for Mutual Futures.

Pages 1 to 3 establish purpose and the three-part principle. Pages 3 to 6 connect capability, productive ownership, livelihoods and intelligence. Pages 6 to 7 develop GAJRA and the invitation. The final page provides references. The new home page follows that reasoning rather than attempting to compress the entire essay into slogans.

Joy must retain its positive content: love, curiosity, belonging, creativity, music, rest and discovery. Editing for usefulness does not mean removing emotion. A sentence earns its place when it helps a reader recognise an outcome they want, understand a mechanism, weigh a consequence or take a meaningful next step.

The public site does not need to import every geopolitical, fiscal or medical claim from the essay to explain this connection. Those claims remain available in the original, with their source context.

### Queens: name the work and the authority

Pages 3 to 5 give the useful sequence: gather people, identify work, develop leaders through responsibility. Page 6 distinguishes the fictional AI mentor concepts from the human leadership programme. Pages 7 to 8 retain the breadth of the enterprise catalogue. Pages 9 to 14 connect infrastructure, care, backing, the historical financial guide, a shared home and international growth.

The language is strongest when it identifies customers, teams, budgets, decisions and delivery. Mutual Futures should use the same discipline. An invitation to lead must say what responsibility and support are involved. Counting founders or conferring titles cannot substitute for authority.

The 500 Australian, 10,000 international and 50,000 global ambitions are starting minimums, not caps. The enterprise scope includes unconventional manufacturing, materials, science and space alongside everyday needs. The rewrite preserves that scope rather than reducing the concept to a small-business support directory.

### C-Hour: keep the participant's life in view

The current planning edition includes household care, neighbours, visiting and remote contributions. It does not limit participation to registered organisations or hosted events. Its proposed interaction begins with conversation and personally controlled information, not compulsory administration.

The distinction between money, voluntary time and outcomes does real explanatory work. It prevents a recorded hour from silently becoming an investment balance or proof of benefit. Equally, removing monetary conversion must not remove ordinary thanks, reciprocity or community-chosen rewards.

The prose becomes less effective when multiple paragraphs repeat that participants could shape future possibilities. State the provisional status where it matters, then identify the decision people can actually shape: what is recorded, how it is confirmed, who can see it and whether the activity becomes easier. The new chapter uses a repair-session example to explain the three records without inventing a pilot, a dollar amount or an operating service.

The Braided Economy is a whole-life idea. C-Hour recognises voluntary contribution; it does not fund the rest of life. Income, paid learning, care, creativity and Intermittent Retirement must stay connected to Mutual Futures' broader arrangements.

## Language changes in Mutual Futures

| Previous wording or pattern | Why it underperformed | Revision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| “An idea to explore together. Read the proposal, try the tools and follow the sources.” on every chapter | Uses prominent space without explaining that chapter or advancing the argument | Removed the repeated strip; added direct section navigation |
| “Different places. Shared possibilities.” | Announces a mood but not the proposed relationship | Explain what communities can exchange and which decisions remain theirs |
| Business succession as the homepage opening and the only roadmap starting point | Gives the reader the wrong boundary for the project | Start with the global transition; offer leadership, contribution, technical work and succession as routes |
| Ring geometry and the canonical consciousness mechanism in the first Aura explanation | Introduces specialist architecture before a reader understands its purpose | Explain personal tasks, reflection and permissions first; retain a route to the technical project |
| A slogan-like statement after an already complete explanation | Repeats a sentiment without adding a decision, mechanism or useful invitation | Removed these repeated standalone statements across the chapters |
| Generic invitations to explore | Leaves willing readers without a useful first contribution | Ask for a need, people affected, an offer and the next decision; provide actual email and public correction routes |

Editorial rule for further changes: prefer a concrete subject and verb, explain project names when first introduced, and give each paragraph one job. Use a qualification where it changes the reader's understanding of status or consequences. Avoid surrounding every proposal with the same disclaimer.

## Layout analysis

The Queens PDF gives substantial ideas a controlled, single-column sequence. Its role and budget tables help comparison; its enterprise catalogue belongs in a detailed source rather than in a dense homepage. The live leadership chapter's direct treatment of authority is useful, but its whole site should not be copied as a template.

The UNGA PDF's two-column essay layout holds a coherent argument in eight pages but is too dense to reproduce directly on a phone. The live site improves navigation with chapter divisions and in-page contents. Its compact supplementary text is inconsistent with Mutual Futures' requirement that all visible text remain at least paragraph size.

The C-Hour document works as a planning manual, including the corrections and references that explain changes between editions. Its length is justified for close reading but not as the first encounter. The live chapter structure helps readers choose a topic; its dark visual treatment is not suitable for the light, clearly visible imagery Luke has requested here.

Mutual Futures had the opposite opening problem: an image could occupy most of the first screen before the reader reached the purpose. The revision places each title and introduction before the image. Artwork still spans the full viewport at its natural proportions; it is not cropped, darkened or forced into a short banner. New explanatory chapters do not receive decorative filler images simply to satisfy a template.

Other changes:

- Primary navigation now exposes GAJRA, ownership, work and life, participation and the library.
- In-page links make long chapters navigable without small labels.
- Section sources sit in expandable notes, close to the relevant claim without interrupting every reading passage.
- The existing single font, four text sizes and 18-pixel minimum remain.
- Current documents appear before older source guides. A missing historical original remains visibly missing.

## Artwork review and replacement

The earlier aviation image combined a large multirotor airframe with a separate winged biomimetic device. The collection distracted from the handover and left structural relationships hard to read. The robotics scene combined a full humanoid, a quadruped and a rover; the marine scene combined a ferry, survey craft and fish-like machinery. Their visual busyness concealed uncertainty rather than explaining the work.

Four replacement images now use one principal machine per scene:

| Scene | Replacement subject | What it communicates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aviation | A conventional fixed-wing uncrewed aircraft undergoing a battery inspection | A recognisable manufacturing and maintenance task, with knowledge transfer |
| Robotics | A humanoid upper body secured to a test fixture | Assembly and learning without an ambiguous crowd of moving robots |
| Marine | A small survey catamaran on maintenance cradles | A coherent serviceable platform instead of several unrelated concepts |
| Space | A four-wheel rover prototype beside a test bed | A bounded development task rather than an already delivered space mission |

The replacements are still generated concept images, not engineering evidence. Visual inspection does not validate propulsion, controls or structural performance. Humanoid and non-human biomimicry remain explicit research directions in the text; they do not have to be crammed into every picture. Australian food, construction, health, water, energy, logistics, design and manufacturing scenes retain the breadth of industry requested.

The four originals, optimised web images and exact prompts are recorded in [generated-visuals.md](generated-visuals.md). The built-in image generation tool was used. Old artwork remains archived and is no longer used in those four placements.

## Implemented information flow

1. **Home:** the global transition, worthwhile lives, GAJRA's three questions and connected systems.
2. **GAJRA:** human self-alignment, AI alignment, plural participation and learning from consequences.
3. **Ownership and succession:** productive assets, member decisions and knowledge handovers.
4. **Queens and capital:** leadership authority, enterprise and the resources needed to act.
5. **Work, income and C-Hour:** paid learning, adequate income, contribution, care and time.
6. **Personal AI, civic decisions and shared infrastructure:** permissions, capability and practical tests.
7. **Wider horizons:** resilience, space, travel, regional exchange and culture.
8. **Roadmap and participation:** concrete starting briefs, outcomes and routes to contribute.
9. **Library and sources:** originals, provenance and the earlier development record.

Readers can enter through a specific need rather than completing the whole sequence. Existing page addresses and tools remain available. The source-backed model explanations still precede their tools; the acquisition calculator still leaves unverified business assumptions blank.

## Matters still requiring real participants and evidence

The site can explain a proposal, preserve sources and offer planning tools. It cannot establish a mutual's membership rules, operating finance, paid placements, a C-Hour verification service or the safety of an AI system by describing them. Those require people with actual responsibilities, evidence and agreements.

The useful next step is consequently a specific starting brief, not a larger list of promises. That brief can lead to a scoped trial or a better question. Its results should then change the design.
