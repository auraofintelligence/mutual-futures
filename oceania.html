Mutual Futures / Oceania & Native Nations

Different places.
Shared possibilities.

Communities across Oceania and beyond could share useful ideas, skills and resources while making their own decisions. Each community and First Nation would choose whether to take part and on what terms.

Public proposal

Share ideas while keeping local control

The related P4A Oceania project explores exchanges beginning around Minjerribah and extending across the region. Participating communities could adapt useful ideas while keeping their own governance, languages and priorities. No community is assumed to have joined.

Source trail: P04 · P4A Oceania D05 · Fiji-Australia Vuvale Union Submission D06 · Ocean of Peace Alliance: Veitacini Treaty Submission

First Nations decide for themselves

The Native Nations project centres each Nation's own name, authority and choice of participation. A Nation might share a public teaching method while keeping cultural knowledge private. Sharing one contribution would not imply joining a business organisation.

Nations are not subsidiaries. Personal and collective permissions remain different kinds of authority.

Source trail: P05 · Native Nations of the World D09 · The B.E.L.A.U. Protopian Gambit

Keep clear records of what is shared

The later Vuvale paper separates money, voluntary contribution and wider outcomes such as skills or improved access. Communities could examine that approach on their own terms. A proposed contribution record or treaty arrangement still needs an actual agreement and its legal status established.

Money

Actual funding, earnings, costs, wages, repayments and retained value.

Contribution

Verified voluntary activity and benefits chosen within a defined community arrangement.

Outcomes

Skills, access, resilience, research findings and what people chose to change or stop.

Source trail: D05 · Fiji-Australia Vuvale Union Submission D06 · Ocean of Peace Alliance: Veitacini Treaty Submission

What is GAJRA Earth?

GAJRA stands for the Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance on Earth. It is a proposed connection between people exploring culture, science, learning and how to live well. The image is a garland: distinct flowers joined by a thread, with each keeping its own character. It does not claim to speak for everyone.
