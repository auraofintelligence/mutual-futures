01 / EXPLORE

An ownership transition.
A much larger horizon.

The starting question is practical: could viable businesses with existing staff and clients pass from retiring proprietors into mutual ownership, while their people and operating systems develop together? Luke Nathan Hayes connects that question to adequate livelihoods, lawful civic reflection, health research, existential resilience and freedom to travel, create and rest.

Preserve what people built. Expand what people can become.
