Start with the current direction

The C-Hour plan explains voluntary contribution and corrects earlier currency-like descriptions. Queens explains women’s leadership, enterprise and capital. The UNGA81 contribution connects Joyful Responsible Abundance with capability, ownership and livelihoods.

The older reading guides show how Mutual Futures developed. Their ten original documents are not present in this repository. A guide summarises a source; it does not replace it.
