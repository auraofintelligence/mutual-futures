Check each trip on its own terms

The linked activity atlas is written for Australian passport holders. Entry permission and permission to work, teach, perform or research can be different. Each trip needs current information for the traveller's citizenship, destination and planned activity.

A journey might involveA distinct preparation question
Tourism and time awayEntry, duration, practical support and current destination advice.
Meetings or a conferenceThe intended activity, host invitation and applicable conditions.
Teaching, speaking or creative workWhether that specific activity and any payment are permitted.
Research or a pilotHost arrangements, project responsibilities, data, equipment and intellectual property.
A longer base overseasThe relevant residence, work, tax, insurance and business questions.

Keep a connection to home

A network of workplaces and learning partners could create opportunities to visit, host or collaborate from home. Some people would travel and others would stay local. Arrangements would need to cover support, responsibilities and returning to work.

A break can simply be a break

Time away might mean family, music, a festival, learning a language or doing very little. Intermittent Retirement is intended to leave room for a person's own reasons to take a break.

Travel can create capability. It can also simply be a life someone wants to live.
