01 / EXPLORE

Real work, public learning, voluntary stories.

Strange but True can connect ordinary practical help with filmmaking, music and public explanation. A participating business might document a useful change, a failed experiment or a person's new chapter. Appearing on camera would remain separate from employment or membership.

03 / EXPLORE

2032 and 2035 as project horizons.

Luke's wider documents use Brisbane 2032 as a learning and capability horizon, without this site claiming an official Games partnership. GAJRA Earth proposes a July 2035 convergence around the 50th anniversary of Live Aid, with art, music and reflection across Past, Present and Future. These are proposed project connections, not confirmed event bookings.

04 / EXPLORE

Optimism with a visible record.

The public story would include what people refused, what did not work and what remains financially or technically unresolved. A better-looking dashboard is not the same as a benefit reaching someone's life. The documentary role is to make those differences visible, not conceal them.

More possible lives is the invitation. Real outcomes are the evidence.
