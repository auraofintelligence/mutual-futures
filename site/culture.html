Public proposal

Tell stories about real experiences

A participating business could share a useful change, a failed experiment or something a person has learned. Film and other creative work could make those experiences easier to discuss. Appearing in a story would be a separate choice from working in the business.

What exists

What is imagined

Ideas for 2032 and 2035

The wider proposals use Brisbane 2032 as an opportunity to explore learning and cultural activity, without claiming an official Games partnership. A proposed GAJRA Earth gathering in July 2035 would mark 50 years since Live Aid through art, music and reflection. Neither is a confirmed event programme here.

Share the setbacks too

An honest public record would include ideas people declined, experiments that failed and questions still unresolved. The useful test is what changed in people's lives. A polished presentation cannot answer that on its own.

More possible lives is the invitation. Real outcomes are the evidence.
