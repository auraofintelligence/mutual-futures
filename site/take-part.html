Mutual Futures / Take part

Bring a need, a skill or a practical proposal.

Help turn the ideas into work that people can assess. Start with one situation you understand and the people whose lives it could improve.

Write a short starting brief

Describe the need and the people affected. Say what you can offer, what decision or help is needed next, and what result would make the effort worthwhile. Include any known resources or costs; leave unknowns open.

You can use those four points in a conversation or email. A short, specific contribution is enough to begin.

Business owners and workers

Describe the handover or upgrade, the knowledge to preserve and the people who should shape it.

↗

Prospective leaders and mentors

Describe an enterprise challenge, the authority required and the learning or support you can offer.

↗

Carers and community participants

Describe help already happening and whether recognition would make participation easier.

↗

Engineers, researchers and builders

Describe a useful task, a design or test, the capability available and what remains unproven.

↗

Discuss a proposal or share a correction

Email Luke Nathan Hayes at auraofintelligence@gmail.com to discuss an idea. For a public correction, include the page, the passage and a source or explanation of what should change. Keep private personal and business records out of public issues.

Start with reflection if the next step is unclear

Use the GAJRA questions to identify a change that matters in your own life or community. Consider what would improve, who would carry the costs and who should gain access. Agreement with every part of Mutual Futures is not required.

What this invitation can lead to

The next step may be a clearer problem, a connection between people, a revised design or a scoped trial with agreed responsibilities. Funding, employment, membership and delivery arrangements would require actual agreements. This contact route is not an application to an operating fund or programme.
