Related projects and independent sources

The related projects are work by Luke Nathan Hayes and collaborators. They explain where the ideas came from. Official and scientific sources provide separate information that readers can use to check particular claims.

What the labels mean

Proposal

A design, intention or desired outcome, not a statement that it already exists.

Prototype

A limited working tool with its actual inputs, functions and omissions stated.

Illustration

A calculation or scenario based on explicitly invented assumptions.

Source record

A document or observation whose origin and credits and date remain visible.

Research question

An unresolved mechanism, model or test, without a guaranteed answer.

Project horizon

An ambition or proposed milestone, not a confirmed public event or approval.
