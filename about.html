About Mutual Futures

Mutual Futures is Luke Nathan Hayes’s proposal for global systems change during the AI and automation transition, guided by Joyful Responsible Abundance. It connects productive ownership with income, learning, care, personal agency and time for life.

Why these ideas belong together

Technology changes what people can produce, but its benefits depend on who owns it, who can learn to use it and how decisions are made. Mutual Futures brings those questions together. Business succession is one practical opportunity within a wider transition that includes new enterprises, public services and shared infrastructure.

GAJRA Earth provides the guiding principle for human self-alignment and AI alignment. The Queens plan contributes women’s leadership and enterprise development. C-Hour contributes recognition of voluntary human effort. The linked UNGA81 paper sets out the wider argument as Luke’s personal contribution.

What exists today

This website, its reading guides and four simple browser tools are available now. The proposed mutual business network has not been established here. The tools use your inputs or clearly labelled examples; they are not connected to banks, employers, health services or live public systems.

Your information on this site

There are no accounts, payment forms, advertising or analytics scripts. Tool entries stay in the page unless you choose to save them in your browser or download a copy.
