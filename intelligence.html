01 / EXPLORE

A personal architecture, not an employer profile.

Luke describes Aura of Intelligence as a developing cognitive architecture and digital twin of body and mind. The standard geometry is seven horn tori, each with a 12 by 24 matrix; alternative resolutions remain part of the design. The inside is private and the outside permissioned for sharing. People associate and combine their own facets, programmes and loops rather than receive one compulsory categorisation.

The Aura of Intelligence Keystone of Consciousness remains the canonical name of Luke's proposed mechanism. This site does not claim that machine consciousness or the physical embodiment has been demonstrated.

02 / EXPLORE

The legal work exists beside the app.

Luke reports that the legal layer has not yet been integrated into the Aura app demo. The Australian Law 2012 project publishes the method and design; the Australian Legal Engine repository separately documents implemented retrieval and provision-tracing functions. Future integration is a workstream, not a missing history.

Source trail: P08 · Australian Law 2012: Luke's Relevance P09 · Australian Legal Engine

Draft builder, not access control

My permissions, in plain language

Express a proposed boundary without submitting personal records. The export is a draft for discussion and implementation, not a legal instrument or cryptographic guarantee.

No personal data is sent to a model or server by this tool. Applicable retention requirements, collective authority and the limits of revocation need their own treatment in an actual implementation.

03 / EXPLORE

Separate permissions for separate purposes.

A person may distinguish inference from training, local use from external sharing, temporary processing from retention, and personal records from professional or collective material. The builder below expresses those distinctions as a portable draft. It does not enforce access controls, implement cryptography or create a legally sufficient consent arrangement.

01

Personal

The person's own values, memories, choices and preferred models.

02

Professional

The qualifications and relevant information a role genuinely requires.

03

Collective

Material whose custodians or communities determine the relevant authority and permissions.

04 / EXPLORE

Humans, AI and robots in an intelligible relationship.

In the proposed operating model, a task has a purpose, authorisation, required competence, inputs, resources, dependencies, review and recovery path. Roles can change as capabilities develop. More automation does not by itself resolve who is responsible for an outcome.

Coherence is not one central mind. It is a relationship people can understand, negotiate and revise.
